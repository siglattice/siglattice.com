A personal life operating system

life.siglattice.com

A working system created by Kristopher Williams to give everyday signals durable context. Atlas connects small, observable services across calendars, tasks, documents, home signals, and personal memory so they can become useful without becoming opaque.

Capture once

Keep the source intact, record its provenance, and avoid multiplying copies without purpose.

Context over time

Durable domains let facts, interpretations, and corrections accumulate without pretending they are the same thing.

Useful at the surface

Small services turn that context into timely views and actions while remaining inspectable underneath.
