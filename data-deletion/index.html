Data Deletion

Effective July 28, 2026

Disconnect provider access

You can stop future Meta, Facebook, or Instagram access by removing Siglattice Atlas from Facebook's Apps and Websites settings. Google access can be removed through your Google Account's connected-app permissions.

Removing provider authorization stops future access. It does not by itself remove records already retained by Siglattice for continuity, security, or action history.

Request deletion

Email krwill@fastmail.com with the subject Siglattice data deletion. Include the Facebook or Instagram username whose data should be removed and enough information to verify that you control the account.

The operator will acknowledge the request and remove associated OAuth credentials and locally retained provider data within 30 days, except for minimal records that must be retained for security, legal obligations, or proof of completed deletion.

Private operation

Siglattice is a privately operated personal system and is not open for public registration. At present, connected accounts are controlled or explicitly authorized by the operator.
