Data Deletion

Effective July 28, 2026

Disconnect provider access

Siglattice Atlas is Kristopher Williams's private integration. Kristopher can stop future Meta, Facebook, or Instagram access by removing it from Facebook's Apps and Websites settings. Google access can be removed through the connected-app permissions of the relevant Google Account.

Removing provider authorization stops future access. It does not by itself remove records already retained by Siglattice for continuity, security, or action history.

Request deletion

Kristopher normally manages deletion directly because no outside users connect accounts to Siglattice. If you nevertheless believe information associated with you is present, email krwill@fastmail.com with the subject Siglattice data deletion and enough information to identify and verify the relevant account.

The request will be acknowledged and associated OAuth credentials and locally retained provider data will be removed within 30 days, except for minimal records that must be retained for security, legal obligations, or proof of completed deletion.

Private operation

Siglattice has no public registration or customer accounts. Connected accounts are owned, managed, or explicitly authorized by Kristopher.
